Performance Culture

A short survey for the leadership group

Before you begin

Please answer as candidly as you can. Think about how the leadership group actually works from one week to the next, and not about how it is described in presentations or how you would like it to work. There are no right answers, and a first instinct is usually the most honest one.

The survey takes about seven minutes: 24 statements and two short questions in your own words.

Your answers are anonymous. No names, email addresses or IP addresses are stored with your answers, and nobody can see what any one person said. Only the combined picture of the whole group will be shared, and no result is ever reported for a group of fewer than five people. The combined picture is used only to help the group understand how it works together.
